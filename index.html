What matters to us

Our core values

In the order we hold ourselves to them.

  1. 01

    Safety First

    Every activity is supervised and age-appropriate. We know the basics of keeping toddlers and young kids safe, indoors and out, and we don't take shortcuts.

    Non-negotiable
  2. 02

    Fun & Engagement

    We want every kid genuinely having a blast — games, crafts, pretend play, story time, whatever gets them excited. Bored kids aren't the goal; happy, busy ones are.

  3. 03

    Warmth & Patience

    Every kid gets our patience, no matter what kind of day they're having. Big feelings just mean they need a little extra kindness — so that's what we lead with, always.

  4. 04

    Reliability

    If we say we're available, we mean it. We show up on time and communicate clearly if anything ever changes.

Questions parents ask

A few things to know

How quickly will I hear back after sending a request?

Usually within a day, though it can occasionally take a bit longer. If your date is coming up soon, feel free to follow up.

What if my plans change or I need to cancel?

Just let us know as soon as you can.

Can you watch siblings or more than one kid at a time?

Yes, and pricing is still the same with 2 kids.

Do I need to book far in advance?

The more notice the better, but book whenever you can.

What if something changes on your end?

We'll always tell you as early as possible if anything comes up that affects a booking.
